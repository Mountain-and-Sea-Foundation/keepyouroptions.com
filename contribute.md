# Write for Us

Keep Your Options Open is a Mountain and Sea Foundation program on financial literacy for growers. We publish guest posts from foundation board members, foundation friends, and Our Planty community members who have a story, a lesson, or a strategy worth sharing.

## What we're looking for

- **Personal finance or investing topics**, written in plain language rather than jargon. Think: a lesson learned, a mistake you'd tell a friend to avoid, a strategy that changed how you think about money.
- **The overlap between growing and investing**, when it's there. Funding a garden or a small plant-selling business, budgeting for tools and land, the patience both require. This isn't required for every post, but it's the connective thread of this program, so lean into it where it fits naturally.
- **First-person experience over general advice.** We're not trying to be a source of professional financial guidance (see our disclaimer below), we're a record of what real people are learning as they go.

## What we're not looking for

- Promotional or sales-oriented posts for a specific product, broker, or service
- Generic "5 tips for investing" content that could run on any finance site
- Specific buy/sell recommendations on individual stocks or options

## Format

- **Length:** 600 to 1,200 words is the sweet spot. Shorter is fine if the point lands; longer needs a good reason.
- **Structure:** a short intro, a few `##` subheadings if it's more than a couple hundred words, and a closing thought. Look at existing posts on the site for tone and pacing.
- **Tags:** let us know a category or two (for example, `investing`, `gardening`, `options`, `budgeting`) so we can file it correctly.

## Byline and disclosure

Every guest post is published with your name and a one-line bio, plus a badge noting your affiliation (Mountain and Sea Foundation or Our Planty Community) so readers know who's writing. If you'd like to link to your own site or the Our Planty profile, include that with your pitch.

## How to submit

**Email a draft or a pitch.** Send it to `hello@keepyouroptions.com` with a subject line like "Guest post: [your idea]." Include:

- Your name and how you'd like your bio to read
- Your affiliation (foundation board, foundation friend, or Our Planty, and how)
- A draft, or a two or three sentence pitch if you'd like to talk it through first

If you're comfortable with GitHub, you're also welcome to open a pull request directly against the site's repository with your post as a markdown file. Either route works, use whichever is easier for you.

## What happens next

We read every submission and reply within a couple of weeks. If it's a fit, we'll work with you on any edits and let you know before it goes live. If it's not quite right for this program, we'll tell you why and, where we can, suggest a change that might make it work.
